Abstract. I am a PhD student at the University of Ljubljana, supervised by Prof. Daniel Smertnig. I work in noncommutative ring theory. My thesis is on one-sided ideal theory in hereditary Noetherian prime rings. Earlier I worked on images of noncommutative polynomials and the L’vov–Kaplansky conjecture.

MSC 2020: 16E60 (hereditary rings), 16N60 (prime rings), 16P40 (Noetherian rings), 19A49 (K0 of rings), 16R99 (polynomial identities), 16S50 (matrix rings).
